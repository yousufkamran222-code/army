# Stickstorm demo guide (for Yousuf)

Play link (after the one-time Pages setup in the README): **https://yousufkamran222-code.github.io/army/**
It works on a laptop and on a phone. On a phone, hold it **sideways** for battles. The shop works either way.

## 1. How to play (under 10 lines)

1. **Shop:** spend gold on **units** (more stickmen; 3 copies = Lv 2, 9 copies = Lv 3) and **relics** (passive combo effects). Tap or hover anything to see what it does.
2. **Drag army cards to reorder them.** Slot #1 deploys first and fights at the front. Drag relics too: **Echo Stone** doubles the relic to its right.
3. Check the **"You vs Them" bar** and the enemy preview, then press **Fight**.
4. The battle plays itself. You get **2 commands** (buttons at the bottom, or keys 1–4). Timing them is your job: Charge as the lines meet, Rally after a bad trade.
5. Watch the **relic icons pulse** with labels like "Split ×24" when your combo fires. 1×/2×/4× and ⏸ are at the top.
6. Win gold (fast or flawless wins pay extra, and you get +1 interest per 5 gold you hold). Lose once and the run is over.
7. Beat the **Warlord** (battle 4) and the **Siege Golem** (battle 8) to win the run.

## 2. Three builds to try

Look for these pieces in the shop. Reroll (🎲) if needed. If you just want to *see* a build, open the debug menu (🐞) → **Quick builds**.

**A. Swarm flood (the biggest numbers)**
- Units: lots of **Swarmlings**, put in **slot #1**. Add a couple of Shieldbearers and Archers.
- Relics: **Mitosis** (dying Swarmlings split into 2), **Brood Mother** (×1.5 Swarmlings), **Vanguard Banner** (slot #1 doubled), then **Strength in Numbers** (+1% damage per living ally).
- What it feels like: a green-and-orange tide. It can pass 1,500 stickmen by battle 8. Swarm Burst (unlocked by Mitosis) splits every Swarmling at once.

**B. Necromancer army (grow by killing)**
- Units: **Necromancers** behind Shieldbearers and Swordsmen.
- Relics: **Soul Harvest** (2 minions per corpse), **Gravedigger** (raise twice as fast), **Grave Pact** (minions get Blade bonuses), **War Drums** (Blade ×1.3), **Lich Crown** (minions explode, and you get the Raise Dead command).
- What it feels like: the fight gets *bigger* as it goes on, because both sides' dead stand back up on your side.

**C. Arrow storm (volley scaling)**
- Units: a wall of **Shieldbearers** (and Spearmen) in front, **Archers** behind.
- Relics: **Shield Quiver** (+1 arrow per 4 Shield stickmen, up to +10 per volley), **Eagle Eye**, **Hail of Arrows** (arrows that kill split into more arrows), and **Echo Stone** placed *left* of Shield Quiver.
- What it feels like: the sky goes dark. The Volley command fires double arrows from everyone at once.

(Bonus: **Giants + Catapult Arms + Titan Blood** throw enemy stickmen into their own army.)

## 3. Debug / cheat menu

Click the **🐞 button** (top right in the shop, bottom right in battle) or press the **` (backtick) key**. It lets you:
- jump to any battle (1–8, bosses marked 👑)
- add gold (+10/+50/+200)
- add 3 copies of any unit
- grant any relic (it replaces the leftmost one if you already have 5)
- load a quick build (Swarm / Necro / Volley / Giants)
- win the current battle instantly

Fast way to see late-game chaos: 🐞 → Quick builds → **Swarm** → jump to **8** → Fight → try **4×**.

## 4. Questions to answer after playing

1. Did I want **"one more run"** when it ended? Why, or why not?
2. Did my build feel like **my** idea, or did the shop decide for me?
3. Was watching the battle **exciting**, or just waiting? Did the commands keep me involved?
4. Did the **snowball moment** (hundreds of stickmen, relic labels popping) feel amazing? When exactly did it happen?
5. Could I tell **why** I won or lost from what I saw on screen?
6. Was there one relic or unit I'd **always** pick? Any I'd **never** pick?
7. What was **boring** or confusing? (Shop, waiting, reading text, a particular battle?)
8. On my phone: was it readable and comfortable?
9. If this were a full game, what's the one thing I'd most want added?
